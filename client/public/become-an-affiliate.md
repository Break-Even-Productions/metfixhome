# Become a MetFix Affiliate

> Bring metabolic health programming to your gym and join the MetFix affiliate network.

URL: https://metfix.org/become-an-affiliate
Application: https://metfix.fillout.com/affiliate-application

## Who should apply

Gym owners, box owners, and facility operators who want to implement MetFix metabolic health programming for their community.

## What affiliates get

- Access to MetFix online courses at no cost
- Resource Library (streams, journal clubs, articles, whiteboards, book reports)
- Meal planning and client tracking features on the MetFix app
- Revenue share when clients buy MetFix courses through the gym
- Brand license, affiliate dashboard, and gym finder listing

## Requirements path

1. Complete MetFix Essentials Advanced (online; required to apply)
2. Submit the affiliate application
3. Foundations Seminar (two-day in person) is strongly recommended and may be completed after affiliation

## Pricing note

Public site copy has listed affiliation around $170 per month with an annual commitment. Confirm current pricing on the live affiliate page or application.
