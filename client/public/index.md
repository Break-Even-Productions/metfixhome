# MetFix

> The metabolic health affiliation for gyms and coaches.

MetFix trains coaches, gym owners, and health leaders to prevent and reverse chronic disease using metabolic science that works in the real world. Affiliates join a network of 130+ gyms (year one) with course access, programming, resource library tools, brand licensing, and revenue share opportunities.

MetFix is an education platform, not a certification body. Co-founded by Emily Kaplan and Greg Glassman. Research foundation: The Broken Science Initiative.
